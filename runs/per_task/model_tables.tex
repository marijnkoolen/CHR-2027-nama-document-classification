\begin{table}[t]
\centering
\caption{Training regimes: how backbone features are turned into a prediction.}
\label{tab:regimes}
\small
\begin{tabular}{llp{7cm}}
\toprule
Regime & Trainable component & Description \\
\midrule
Baseline & $k$-NN / XGBoost &
  Frozen backbone feature(s) (single, or L2-normalised concatenation for multimodal) fed directly to a classical classifier. \\
Sequence & BiLSTM (2$\times$256, dropout 0.3) &
  A dossier's per-page frozen features modeled as a sequence: 512-d projection, bidirectional LSTM, page-level softmax head. \\
Early fusion & MLP (512-512, dropout 0.3) &
  Frozen image and text features separately projected to 512-d, concatenated, and passed through a 2-layer MLP head. \\
Late fusion & None (derived) &
  Softmax outputs of one independently fine-tuned vision model and one independently fine-tuned text model, averaged - no training or architecture of its own. \\
Finetune & Backbone + linear head &
  Backbone weights updated end-to-end (VGG16/EfficientNet-B0 use a fixed unfreeze policy; other backbones unfreeze the last 2 blocks/layers by default). \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}
